01 Collection of your information

We may collect information about you in a variety of ways. The information we collect depends on the content and materials you use, and includes:

Personal or account information

When you use our apps or services, you may sign in or create an account to track and sync your gaming progress and other activities across devices. You may be asked for your name, email address, and password. You are responsible for keeping your account information correct and up to date.

Device information

We may collect device details such as the type of device, operating system, application version, device ID, advertising ID, language, time zone, and IP address.

Usage data

We may automatically collect information about how you interact with the Services and your activities, such as crash data, location data, in-app purchases, and other usage data.

Location and other information

We may request access to location-based information from your mobile device to provide location-based services. You can change permissions in your device settings. Some features, such as camera features in a game, may require camera access.

Information from third parties

We may receive information about you from third-party service providers that collect information through our Services in accordance with their own privacy policies. See Appendix B for providers.

Social networks

If you create an account using Facebook or Google, you authorize that provider to share information such as your name, profile picture, email address, language preference, and other information listed in the authorization.

Information you provide

We collect information you provide when using our apps or services, including language preferences or information provided for surveys, contests, tournaments, or events. You are responsible for keeping it accurate.

Cookies and similar technologies

We and our advertising partners may use cookies, mobile SDKs, and similar technologies to personalize advertising, understand how our Services are used, and improve or market our Services. See Appendices A and B for more information.

02 How we use your personal data

Accurate information helps us provide a smooth, efficient, and customized experience. We may use information collected through the Services to:

Provide our Services

Provide apps, features, purchases, customer service, and other services you request; process transactions; and sync data across devices signed in to your account.

Safeguard and protect our Services

Verify identity, provide customer service, protect security, combat spam, malware, and malicious activity, monitor fraud, maintain backups, and protect our products and services.

Promote our Services

Contact you about surveys, support, and how you use our services so we can improve our offerings.

Send push notifications

Keep you updated about changes, new features, and services. You can turn off notifications in your phone settings.

Provide personalized advertisements

Use third-party advertising networks to provide personalized ads. In the European Economic Area, Norway, and Switzerland, some networks rely on your consent to process personal data. See the targeted advertising instructions below to opt out or withdraw consent.

Meet legal obligations

Respond to legal proceedings and other obligations or requirements under applicable law.

03 How we keep your personal information

We keep personal information only as long as necessary for the purposes in this Privacy Policy or to comply with applicable law. We may retain information to meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, enforce agreements and policies, and improve our apps and services through internal analysis.

Personal information collected through our apps and services will be deleted when it no longer serves one of these purposes, and in any event after fourteen months of inactivity in your use of our Services. After deletion, you may no longer be able to exercise rights of access, erasure, rectification, or data portability.

04 Your rights and options

You may have the right to correct or delete your data, object to or restrict how we use or share it, and withdraw consent where you previously provided consent. For example, you can turn off camera access in your mobile device settings. Withdrawal does not affect processing that took place before withdrawal.

Opt out of targeted advertising

We and our advertising partners may use advertising identifiers, including Android Advertising ID and Apple ID for Advertising (IDFA), and similar technologies to provide and optimize advertising.

For iOS devices

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Select Privacy.
  3. Select Advertising and enable Limit Ad Tracking (iOS 13 and older), or select Tracking and turn off Allow Apps to Request to Track (iOS 14).

For Android devices

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Select Google.
  3. Select Ads and enable Opt out of Ads Personalization.

After opting out, networks will show contextual rather than interest-based ads where possible. You may still see non-customized advertising and personalized ads from networks that obtained consent through other apps or websites.

Requests to exercise your rights should be emailed to support@ktsdigital.com. Requests are free of charge and will be addressed as early as possible. You may also contact your local data protection authority within the European Economic Area.

06 Children

Our Services are not intended for children under sixteen (16). We will not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under sixteen without parental consent. If you are under sixteen, please do not provide information. Parents and guardians should spend time with children online and review the sites they visit. If you believe we may have received information from a child, contact us immediately at support@ktsdigital.com.

07 Links to other third-party sites

Our Privacy Policy may contain links to third-party sites that are not operated by us. Review the privacy policy of any third-party site before visiting it. We have no control over the content or practices of those sites.

08 California privacy rights

We do not sell personal information as those terms are defined in the California Consumer Privacy Act. California consumers may use the Digital Advertising Alliance’s tool at privacyrights.info or its AppChoices mobile application to limit collection and opt out of participating companies’ interest-based advertising.

California residents may ask us for a list of third parties with whom we share personal information for marketing purposes. To request this notice, email support@ktsdigital.com with your name, account information, proof of California residence, and the subject line “My California Privacy Rights.”

09 Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may change this Privacy Policy at any time and for any reason. We will try to alert you by updating the “Last updated” date. Your continued use of the Services after a revised policy is posted means you accept the changes.

10 Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at support@ktsdigital.com.
